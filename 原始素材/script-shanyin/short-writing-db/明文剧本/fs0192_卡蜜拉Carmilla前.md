## 第1集：床边的女孩

## Episode 1: The Girl Beside the Bed

本集卡点：强入口 + 童年咬痕 + 成年复发。

Episode function: Strong opening + childhood bite + adult recurrence.

### 场景1

夜 / 内 / 古堡儿童卧室 / 十二年前

Night / Interior / Castle Nursery / Twelve Years Earlier

出场人物：幼年洛拉、神秘少女

Characters: Young Laura, Mysterious Girl

△ 古堡深夜。烛火快熄。六岁的洛拉从床上醒来，发现女仆不在。

△ Deep night in the castle. The candle is dying. Six-year-old Laura wakes and finds the maid gone.

洛拉：玛丽？你去哪了？

Laura: Mary? Where did you go?

△ 房间没有回答。衣柜影子在墙上晃动。

△ No answer. The wardrobe shadow sways across the wall.

洛拉：我不喜欢一个人。

Laura: I do not like being alone.

△ 床边慢慢升起一张少女的脸。她美得像从月光里走出来，眼神却安静得不像活人。

△ A young woman's face slowly rises beside the bed. She is as beautiful as moonlight, but her stillness is not quite living.

神秘少女：别哭。

Mysterious Girl: Do not cry.

洛拉：你是谁？

Laura: Who are you?

神秘少女：一个很久以后才会来找你的人。

Mysterious Girl: Someone who will come for you much later.

洛拉：我不认识你。

Laura: I do not know you.

神秘少女：现在不认识。以后会的。

Mysterious Girl: Not now. You will.

△ 她躺到洛拉身边，手掌冰凉，却轻轻拍着洛拉的背。

△ She lies beside Laura. Her hand is cold, but it pats Laura's back gently.

神秘少女：睡吧，小夜莺。

Mysterious Girl: Sleep, little nightingale.

洛拉：只有妈妈这样叫我。

Laura: Only Mother called me that.

神秘少女：所以你更该记住我。

Mysterious Girl: Then you should remember me all the more.

△ 洛拉的眼皮越来越沉。她被一种奇异的温柔安抚住。

△ Laura's eyelids grow heavy. A strange tenderness soothes her.

### 场景2

夜 / 内 / 同一卧室

Night / Interior / Same Nursery

△ 洛拉突然痛醒，像有两根针刺进胸口。

△ Laura wakes in pain, as if two needles have pierced her chest.

洛拉：啊！

Laura: Ah!

△ 神秘少女已经退到床脚。她盯着洛拉，唇边有一点暗红。

△ The mysterious girl has retreated to the foot of the bed. She stares at Laura, a dark red trace on her lips.

洛拉：你咬我？

Laura: Did you bite me?

神秘少女：我只是认出你。

Mysterious Girl: I only recognized you.

△ 门外传来脚步。少女像影子一样滑到床下。

△ Footsteps rush outside. The girl slips under the bed like a shadow.

洛拉：来人！她在这里！

Laura: Help! She is here!

△ 女仆、管家冲进来，翻开床底，什么也没有。

△ The maid and housekeeper rush in and search under the bed. Nothing.

管家：床还是热的。

Housekeeper: The bed is still warm.

女仆：小姐胸口没有伤。

Maid: There is no wound on Miss Laura's chest.

洛拉：有！她刚刚就在这里！

Laura: There is! She was right here!

△ 大人们面面相觑，却不敢再说。

△ The adults exchange looks, afraid to speak further.

### 场景3

夜 / 内 / 洛拉成年后的卧室 / 现在

Night / Interior / Laura's Bedroom / Present Day

出场人物：成年洛拉

Characters: Adult Laura

△ 十九岁的洛拉猛地从梦中惊醒。窗外月光雪白，古堡一片死寂。

△ Nineteen-year-old Laura jolts awake from the dream. Moonlight is white outside; the castle is silent.

洛拉：又是她。

Laura: Her again.

△ 她低头。锁骨下方，皮肤忽然刺痛，像十二年前同一个位置。

△ She looks down. Beneath her collarbone, the same place from twelve years ago suddenly stings.

洛拉：不可能。

Laura: Impossible.

△ 她拉开衣领。两点极淡的红痕慢慢浮现。

△ She pulls her collar open. Two faint red marks slowly appear.

【集尾钩子 / Cliffhanger】

△ 走廊尽头，一个女人的轻笑声响起。

△ From the end of the corridor, a woman's soft laugh echoes.

神秘声音：你终于长大了，小夜莺。

Mysterious Voice: You have finally grown up, little nightingale.

## 第2集：黑马车撞进古堡

## Episode 2: The Black Carriage

本集卡点：伯莎死亡预警 + 外来者事故入局 + 卡蜜拉叫出洛拉名字。

Episode function: Bertha's death warning + outsider enters through accident + Carmilla calls Laura by name.

### 场景1

傍晚 / 外 / 古堡林道

Dusk / Exterior / Forest Road Before the Castle

出场人物：洛拉、父亲

Characters: Laura, Father

△ 洛拉和父亲沿着林道散步。远处是废弃的 Karnstein 村落，教堂屋顶已经塌了。

△ Laura and her father walk along the forest road. In the distance lies the abandoned Karnstein village, its church roof collapsed.

洛拉：将军明天真的会带伯莎来吗？

Laura: Is the General truly bringing Bertha tomorrow?

父亲：不会了。

Father: No.

洛拉：他改期了？

Laura: Has he delayed the visit?

父亲：伯莎死了。

Father: Bertha is dead.

△ 洛拉停住。她从没见过伯莎，却已经期待这个同龄朋友很久。

△ Laura stops. She has never met Bertha, but she has hoped for this friend for weeks.

洛拉：怎么会？信里不是说她只是身体虚弱？

Laura: How? The letter only said she was weak.

父亲：将军说，害死她的不是病。

Father: The General says illness did not kill her.

洛拉：那是什么？

Laura: Then what did?

△ 父亲打开信。字迹凌乱，几乎划破纸面。

△ Her father opens the letter. The handwriting is frantic, almost cutting through the paper.

父亲：他说，有个美丽、温柔、看似无害的女孩进入他家。然后伯莎一天天衰弱。

Father: He says a beautiful, gentle, harmless-seeming girl entered his house. Then Bertha weakened day by day.

洛拉：一个女孩？

Laura: A girl?

父亲：他最后写了一句：如果这样的女孩来到你家，千万不要收留。

Father: His last warning is this: if such a girl comes to your house, do not take her in.

### 场景2

夜 / 外 / 古堡吊桥前

Night / Exterior / Before the Castle Drawbridge

出场人物：洛拉、父亲、佩罗东夫人、拉方丹小姐

Characters: Laura, Father, Madame Perrodon, Mademoiselle De Lafontaine

△ 月亮升起。四人站在吊桥边。林道突然传来马蹄和车轮声。

△ The moon rises. The four stand by the drawbridge. Hooves and wheels suddenly thunder from the forest road.

拉方丹小姐：这个时间，怎么会有马车？

Mademoiselle De Lafontaine: At this hour? Who would be traveling?

△ 一辆黑色马车冲过石桥，四匹马像疯了一样奔向古堡。

△ A black carriage bursts over the stone bridge, four horses racing toward the castle as if mad.

车内女声：救命！

Voice in Carriage: Help!

父亲：退后！

Father: Stand back!

△ 马车撞上路边老树，轰然翻倒。灯笼碎裂，火星和月光一起落在泥地上。

△ The carriage strikes an old roadside tree and overturns. Lantern glass shatters; sparks and moonlight scatter across the mud.

### 场景3

夜 / 外 / 翻倒的马车旁

Night / Exterior / Beside the Overturned Carriage

出场人物：洛拉、父亲、伯爵夫人、卡蜜拉

Characters: Laura, Father, Countess, Carmilla

△ 一个穿黑天鹅绒长裙的贵妇从车边站起，脸色苍白，眼神却冷静。

△ A noblewoman in black velvet rises beside the carriage, pale but unnervingly calm.

伯爵夫人：救我的女儿。

Countess: Save my daughter.

△ 仆人从车里抱出一个少女。她黑发披散，像睡着，又像死去。

△ Servants carry out a young woman. Her dark hair spills loose. She looks asleep, or dead.

洛拉：是她。

Laura: It is her.

父亲：你认识她？

Father: You know her?

洛拉：我小时候梦见过她。就在我的床边。

Laura: I dreamed of her when I was a child. Beside my bed.

伯爵夫人：梦会挑选它想要的人。

Countess: Dreams choose whom they want.

父亲：夫人，她必须马上休息。今晚请留在古堡。

Father: Madam, she must rest at once. Stay at the castle tonight.

伯爵夫人：我不能停留。我有一件关乎生死的事，必须立刻去办。

Countess: I cannot stay. I have a matter of life and death to attend to.

洛拉：那她怎么办？

Laura: Then what of her?

伯爵夫人：她留在这里。

Countess: She remains here.

△ 洛拉心头一紧。父亲犹豫，却被少女苍白的样子打动。

△ Laura's heart tightens. Her father hesitates, moved by the girl's pallor.

父亲：我们会照顾她，直到您回来。

Father: We will care for her until you return.

△ 昏迷少女忽然睁眼。她不看任何人，只看洛拉。

△ The unconscious girl suddenly opens her eyes. She looks at no one but Laura.

【集尾钩子 / Cliffhanger】

卡蜜拉：洛拉。

Carmilla: Laura.

洛拉：你怎么知道我的名字？

Laura: How do you know my name?

卡蜜拉：我找了你十二年。

Carmilla: I have been looking for you for twelve years.

## 第3集：别问她从哪里来

## Episode 3: Do Not Ask Where She Came From

本集卡点：外来者入住 + 身份禁令 + 伯爵夫人露出危险话术。

Episode function: The stranger stays + identity prohibition + the Countess reveals danger through language.

### 场景1

夜 / 内 / 古堡大厅

Night / Interior / Castle Hall

出场人物：洛拉、父亲、伯爵夫人、佩罗东夫人、卡蜜拉

Characters: Laura, Father, Countess, Madame Perrodon, Carmilla

△ 卡蜜拉被安置在长沙发上。她的手垂在毯外，白得像雪。

△ Carmilla is placed on a long sofa. Her hand hangs outside the blanket, white as snow.

佩罗东夫人：她的手太冷了。

Madame Perrodon: Her hand is terribly cold.

父亲：受了惊吓，脉搏弱也正常。

Father: A shock can weaken the pulse.

洛拉：可她刚才叫了我的名字。

Laura: But she spoke my name.

伯爵夫人：病中的人会说很多听不懂的话。

Countess: The sick say many things no one should understand.

洛拉：她说她找了我十二年。

Laura: She said she had been looking for me for twelve years.

△ 伯爵夫人第一次正眼看洛拉，像确认一件货物没有损坏。

△ For the first time, the Countess looks directly at Laura, as if confirming that a purchased object is undamaged.

伯爵夫人：那你应该高兴。世上不是每个人都值得被寻找。

Countess: Then you should be pleased. Not everyone in this world is worth seeking.

### 场景2

夜 / 内 / 古堡侧厅

Night / Interior / Castle Antechamber

出场人物：父亲、伯爵夫人

Characters: Father, Countess

△ 伯爵夫人把父亲叫到一旁。洛拉隔着半扇门偷听。

△ The Countess draws Laura's father aside. Laura listens through the half-open door.

父亲：我至少需要知道她的全名。

Father: I must at least know her full name.

伯爵夫人：卡蜜拉。

Countess: Carmilla.

父亲：姓氏呢？

Father: And her family name?

伯爵夫人：她会告诉你们她愿意告诉的。

Countess: She will tell you what she wishes to tell.

父亲：这不合常理。

Father: That is hardly reasonable.

伯爵夫人：常理救不了伯莎。

Countess: Reason did not save Bertha.

△ 父亲脸色一变。

△ Her father's face changes.

父亲：你认识将军的养女？

Father: You knew the General's ward?

伯爵夫人：我认识很多被怜爱耽误的女孩。

Countess: I have known many girls ruined by affection.

父亲：夫人，你到底要去哪里？

Father: Madam, where are you truly going?

伯爵夫人：去阻止一件比死亡更急的事。

Countess: To prevent something more urgent than death.

### 场景3

夜 / 内 / 古堡大厅

Night / Interior / Castle Hall

出场人物：洛拉、伯爵夫人、卡蜜拉

Characters: Laura, Countess, Carmilla

△ 伯爵夫人临行前俯身，贴近卡蜜拉耳边。卡蜜拉还闭着眼。

△ Before leaving, the Countess bends close to Carmilla's ear. Carmilla's eyes remain closed.

伯爵夫人：记住，三个月。

Countess: Remember. Three months.

△ 卡蜜拉的睫毛轻轻一颤。

△ Carmilla's lashes tremble.

伯爵夫人：不要问路，不要说姓氏，不要让他们知道你从哪里来。

Countess: Ask no roads, speak no family name, let them know nothing of where you came from.

洛拉：她为什么不能说？

Laura: Why must she not speak?

伯爵夫人：因为知道过去的人，通常活不到将来。

Countess: Because those who know the past rarely live to see the future.

△ 洛拉僵住。伯爵夫人披上斗篷，准备上车。

△ Laura freezes. The Countess pulls on her cloak and prepares to enter the carriage.

【集尾钩子 / Cliffhanger】

△ 伯爵夫人经过洛拉身边，用只有她能听见的声音说。

△ As the Countess passes Laura, she speaks in a voice only Laura can hear.

伯爵夫人：她喜欢你。别让她太快喝完你。

Countess: She likes you. Do not let her drink you too quickly.

洛拉：这是什么意思？

Laura: What does that mean?

伯爵夫人：意思是，把门锁好。

Countess: It means, lock your door.

## 第4集：我十二年前见过你

## Episode 4: I Saw You Twelve Years Ago

本集卡点：梦境互认 + 危险亲密 + 爱与恐惧同时建立。

Episode function: Mutual dream recognition + dangerous intimacy + attraction and fear established together.

### 场景1

深夜 / 内 / 卡蜜拉客房

Late Night / Interior / Carmilla's Guest Room

出场人物：洛拉、卡蜜拉

Characters: Laura, Carmilla

△ 医生离开后，卡蜜拉坐在床上。烛光照着她的脸。洛拉站在门口，迟迟不敢靠近。

△ After the doctor leaves, Carmilla sits up in bed. Candlelight touches her face. Laura stands in the doorway, afraid to approach.

卡蜜拉：你怕我？

Carmilla: Are you afraid of me?

洛拉：我怕我记得你。

Laura: I am afraid that I remember you.

卡蜜拉：那就过来，看看你记得的是不是这张脸。

Carmilla: Then come closer and see whether this is the face you remember.

△ 洛拉一步步走近，呼吸越来越乱。

△ Laura walks closer, step by step, her breathing unsteady.

洛拉：十二年前，我床边有个女孩。她安慰我，又咬了我。

Laura: Twelve years ago, there was a girl beside my bed. She comforted me, then bit me.

卡蜜拉：在我的梦里，是你先哭。

Carmilla: In my dream, you cried first.

洛拉：那不是梦。

Laura: It was not a dream.

卡蜜拉：如果不是梦，我们早就认识了。

Carmilla: If it was not a dream, then we have known each other for years.

洛拉：你到底是什么人？

Laura: What are you?

卡蜜拉：一个终于回到你身边的人。

Carmilla: Someone who has finally returned to you.

### 场景2

深夜 / 内 / 卡蜜拉客房

Late Night / Interior / Carmilla's Guest Room

△ 洛拉想离开。卡蜜拉忽然握住她的手。那只手冰凉，却有奇异的力量。

△ Laura tries to leave. Carmilla suddenly takes her hand. Her hand is cold, yet strangely powerful.

卡蜜拉：别走。

Carmilla: Do not go.

洛拉：你让我不舒服。

Laura: You make me uneasy.

卡蜜拉：因为你不习惯被需要。

Carmilla: Because you are not used to being needed.

洛拉：我不习惯被一个陌生人这样看着。

Laura: I am not used to being looked at like this by a stranger.

卡蜜拉：陌生人不会知道你小时候怕黑，睡觉要留灯。陌生人也不会知道，你母亲叫你小夜莺。

Carmilla: A stranger would not know you feared the dark as a child, or that you slept with a lamp. A stranger would not know your mother called you little nightingale.

洛拉：你不许再提我母亲。

Laura: You must not speak of my mother again.

卡蜜拉：那你就问我别的。

Carmilla: Then ask me something else.

洛拉：你的姓氏。

Laura: Your family name.

△ 卡蜜拉的笑意淡下去。

△ Carmilla's smile fades.

卡蜜拉：还不到时候。

Carmilla: Not yet.

洛拉：你的家在哪里？

Laura: Where is your home?

卡蜜拉：在西边。

Carmilla: To the west.

洛拉：西边只有废村、墓地和 Karnstein 古堡。

Laura: To the west there is only the ruined village, the graves, and Karnstein Castle.

卡蜜拉：那就别往西边看。

Carmilla: Then do not look west.

### 场景3

深夜 / 内 / 卡蜜拉客房门口

Late Night / Interior / Outside Carmilla's Room

△ 洛拉准备离开。卡蜜拉靠在枕边，虚弱而美丽。

△ Laura prepares to leave. Carmilla leans against the pillows, weak and beautiful.

卡蜜拉：今晚不要让女仆守在我房里。

Carmilla: Do not let a maid sit in my room tonight.

洛拉：你刚醒，应该有人照看。

Laura: You have only just woken. Someone should watch over you.

卡蜜拉：我睡觉时不喜欢有人看我。

Carmilla: I do not like to be watched while I sleep.

洛拉：你怕什么？

Laura: What are you afraid of?

卡蜜拉：盗贼。刺客。还有那些以为锁门就能挡住梦的人。

Carmilla: Robbers. Assassins. And those who think a locked door can keep out dreams.

△ 洛拉握住门把。

△ Laura grips the door handle.

洛拉：你说话总像谜。

Laura: You speak in riddles.

卡蜜拉：因为答案会伤人。

Carmilla: Because answers hurt.

【集尾钩子 / Cliffhanger】

△ 卡蜜拉忽然起身抱住洛拉，贴在她耳边。

△ Carmilla suddenly rises and embraces Laura, whispering against her ear.

卡蜜拉：你会属于我。就算你恨我，也会跟我走到死亡之后。

Carmilla: You will belong to me. Even if you hate me, you will come with me beyond death.

△ 洛拉想推开她，却发现自己全身发软，像被梦拽住。

△ Laura tries to push her away, but her body weakens, as if caught by a dream.

## 第5集：画像上的伯爵夫人

## Episode 5: The Countess in the Portrait

本集卡点：外部死亡蔓延 + 卡蜜拉异常暴露 + Karnstein 画像揭出身份谜团。

Episode function: Death spreads outside + Carmilla's abnormality surfaces + Karnstein portrait reveals identity mystery.

### 场景1

午后 / 外 / 古堡林荫道

Afternoon / Exterior / Castle Lime Walk

出场人物：洛拉、卡蜜拉、送葬队伍

Characters: Laura, Carmilla, Funeral Procession

△ 洛拉陪卡蜜拉散步。卡蜜拉白天总是疲倦，走不了几步便坐下。

△ Laura walks with Carmilla. Carmilla is always languid by day and sits after only a few steps.

洛拉：你睡到中午，吃得又少。这样怎么会好？

Laura: You sleep until noon and barely eat. How can you recover?

卡蜜拉：我需要的，不是你们桌上的东西。

Carmilla: What I need is not found on your table.

洛拉：那你需要什么？

Laura: Then what do you need?

卡蜜拉：靠近你。

Carmilla: To be near you.

△ 远处传来圣歌。村民抬着棺木经过。死者是林中守人的女儿。

△ A hymn rises in the distance. Villagers pass with a coffin. The dead girl is the forester's daughter.

洛拉：她才十几岁。听说她死前也说，有东西夜里掐住她的喉咙。

Laura: She was only a young girl. They say before she died, she claimed something seized her throat at night.

卡蜜拉：别唱了。

Carmilla: Stop singing.

洛拉：他们是在送她最后一程。

Laura: They are seeing her off.

卡蜜拉：那声音像针一样扎进我的耳朵。

Carmilla: That sound pierces my ears like needles.

△ 洛拉跟着低声唱了一句。卡蜜拉猛地抓住她的手腕。

△ Laura softly joins one line of the hymn. Carmilla suddenly grips her wrist.

卡蜜拉：我说，别唱。

Carmilla: I said, stop.

△ 卡蜜拉脸色发青，牙关紧咬，全身发抖。

△ Carmilla's face turns livid. Her teeth clench; her whole body trembles.

洛拉：卡蜜拉！你怎么了？

Laura: Carmilla! What is wrong?

卡蜜拉：都是你们的祈祷，把人勒死。

Carmilla: It is your prayers that strangle people.

### 场景2

傍晚 / 外 / 古堡庭院

Evening / Exterior / Castle Courtyard

出场人物：洛拉、卡蜜拉、游方艺人

Characters: Laura, Carmilla, Mountebank

△ 一个驼背游方艺人进了庭院，狗在吊桥边狂吠不肯靠近。

△ A hunchbacked mountebank enters the courtyard. His dog howls at the drawbridge and refuses to come closer.

游方艺人：漂亮小姐们，要护身符吗？最近林子里有 oupire，像狼一样夜里游荡。

Mountebank: Pretty ladies, would you buy a charm? There is an oupire in these woods, wandering by night like a wolf.

洛拉：Oupire 是什么？

Laura: What is an oupire?

游方艺人：吸人命的东西。把这个别在枕头上，它就不敢靠近。

Mountebank: A thing that drinks life. Pin this to your pillow, and it will not come near.

△ 洛拉买下一张护身符。卡蜜拉也买，却把护身符捏得变形。

△ Laura buys a charm. Carmilla buys one too, but crushes it in her fingers.

游方艺人：这位小姐牙齿真尖。若是疼，我有锉刀，可以磨圆。

Mountebank: Your lady friend's tooth is very sharp. If it pains her, I have a file. I can make it round.

△ 卡蜜拉的眼神瞬间冷下去。

△ Carmilla's gaze turns cold in an instant.

卡蜜拉：你敢侮辱我？

Carmilla: How dare you insult me?

游方艺人：我只是眼尖。

Mountebank: I merely have sharp eyes.

卡蜜拉：那你该祈祷自己别看见更多。

Carmilla: Then pray you see no more.

△ 狗叫得更凶。艺人脸色一变，收起东西匆匆离开。

△ The dog howls louder. The mountebank's face changes; he gathers his things and leaves quickly.

### 场景3

夜 / 内 / 古堡大厅

Night / Interior / Castle Hall

出场人物：洛拉、父亲、卡蜜拉、画作修复师

Characters: Laura, Father, Carmilla, Picture Restorer

△ 格拉茨送来的旧画像被一幅幅拆开。画作大多属于洛拉母亲的匈牙利家族。

△ Old portraits from Gratz are unpacked one by one. Most belong to Laura's mother's Hungarian family.

父亲：还有最后一幅。名单上写的是 Marcia Karnstein，1698年。

Father: One more. The list says Marcia Karnstein, 1698.

△ 画布被掀开。大厅安静下来。

△ The covering is removed. The hall falls silent.

△ 画像中的女子穿旧时代礼服，黑发、苍白、微笑，脖颈边有一颗小痣。

△ The woman in the portrait wears an old-fashioned gown. Dark hair, pale skin, a smile, and a small mole near her throat.

洛拉：这不是 Marcia。

Laura: That is not Marcia.

父亲：你看清楚了？

Father: Can you read it clearly?

洛拉：这里写的是 Mircalla，Karnstein 伯爵夫人。

Laura: It says Mircalla, Countess Karnstein.

△ 所有人看向卡蜜拉。她坐在阴影里，脸色没有半点惊讶。

△ Everyone looks at Carmilla. She sits in the shadows, not at all surprised.

父亲：卡蜜拉，你和 Karnstein 家族有关系？

Father: Carmilla, are you connected to the Karnstein family?

卡蜜拉：很久以前，也许有一点。

Carmilla: Long ago, perhaps a little.

洛拉：这不是有一点。她和你一模一样。

Laura: This is not a little. She looks exactly like you.

卡蜜拉：也许是你太想在我身上看见她。

Carmilla: Perhaps you want too badly to see her in me.

洛拉：我想把这幅画挂在我的房间。

Laura: I want this portrait hung in my room.

△ 卡蜜拉终于笑了，笑得温柔又像得逞。

△ Carmilla finally smiles, tenderly, almost triumphantly.

卡蜜拉：那我就能每晚看着你了。

Carmilla: Then I can watch you every night.

【集尾钩子 / Cliffhanger】

△ 洛拉再看画像角落的金字。烛火一晃，字母像自己换了位置。

△ Laura looks again at the golden letters in the corner. The candle flickers, and the letters seem to rearrange themselves.

△ Mircalla。

△ Mircalla.

△ Carmilla。

△ Carmilla.

洛拉：这是同一个名字。

Laura: It is the same name.

△ 卡蜜拉站在她身后，轻声回答。

△ Carmilla stands behind her and answers softly.

卡蜜拉：现在，你知道得太多了。

Carmilla: Now you know too much.
